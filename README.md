# PARALYSIS 👁️

> **PARALYSIS** is a 2D psychological horror platformer developed with **Godot Engine 4**. You play as a young girl hunted by a blind entity that reacts to her heartbeat.

## Overview

- **Genre:** 2D platformer / psychological horror
- **Target playtime:** 20–30 minutes across 3 chapters
- **Engine:** Godot Engine 4.x
- **Key mechanics:**
  - An invisible stress meter communicated through audio, including an accelerating heartbeat.
  - A blind monster that tracks the character by the rhythm of her heartbeat.
  - Route choices between stressful, dark areas and illuminated areas.
